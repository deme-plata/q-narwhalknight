**Problem**: 82 million displaced people lack clean water (UNHCR data)

**Solution**: Deploy MOF-801 water harvesters in refugee camps

#### Technical Specs
- **Framework**: MOF-801 (Zr-fumarate)
- **Capacity**: 0.3 L/kg/day at 20% humidity
- **Robot Type**: Quantum Jellyfish (MOF builders)
- **Swarm Size**: 100 robots per camp
- **Construction Time**: 48 hours for 10-ton MOF system

#### Economics
- **Capital Cost**: $500,000 per camp (robots + materials)
- **Water Production**: 3,000 L/day (serving 1,000 people)
- **Cost per Person**: $500/year (vs. $2,000/year for trucked water)
- **Revenue Model**:
  - UNHCR contracts: $1,000/person/year
  - **Gross Margin**: 50%
  - **Profit per Camp**: $250,000/year

#### Deployment Strategy
1. **Month 1-2**: Pilot in Dadaab, Kenya (world's largest refugee camp)
2. **Month 3-6**: Scale to 10 camps (East Africa, Middle East)
3. **Month 7-12**: 50 camps globally

**Total Revenue Year 1**: $12.5 million
**Total Profit Year 1**: $6.25 million

#### Impact Metrics (Blockchain-Verified)
- **People served**: 50,000
